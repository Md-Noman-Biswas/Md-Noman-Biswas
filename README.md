## About

I'm a final-year **B.Sc. student in Electrical &amp; Computer Engineering** at
[Rajshahi University of Engineering &amp; Technology (RUET)](https://www.ruet.ac.bd/),
Bangladesh, and a part-time research assistant at ELITE Research Lab.

My work sits between **trustworthy AI**, deep learning, and computer vision. The question
I keep coming back to is what happens to a model once the data stops being clean.
Ultrasound images arrive noisy. Pathology slides shift between scanners. A model that
looks accurate on a benchmark can fail quietly on any of that, so I build systems that
hold up under those conditions and say something honest about their own uncertainty.

Currently working on mechanistic interpretability of neural networks, and my undergraduate
thesis on trustworthy AI in healthcare.

🎓 **I'm applying to research-oriented graduate programs (M.S. / Ph.D.)** in reliable,
interpretable AI. If that fits your group, reach me at
[mdnomanbiswassibly@gmail.com](mailto:mdnomanbiswassibly@gmail.com).
